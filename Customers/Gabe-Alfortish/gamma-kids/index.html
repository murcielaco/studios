GAMMA KIDS is the playroom of GAMMA MUSEUM. The same idea — wearable art made through digital fabrication and 3D printing — now sculpted for the ones who run, jump and climb everything. Supercars, lava and squirrels: three pieces of art, made to be played in.

3D printed Made for kids Coming soon

A Piece Nº 01

Lambo

A supercar for little feet.

Sculpted wheel arches, aerodynamic side vents and a mesh windshield on top: LAMBO turns a pair of shoes into a pair of race cars. Built in one piece through 3D printing, with a low, wide stance made for speeding down the hallway.

Coming soon Follow the launch

B Piece Nº 02

Magma

Lava, frozen mid-flow.

Layer after layer of rippling lines wrap the whole shoe, like lava cooling into stone. MAGMA is soft in shape and bold in color — a slip-on sculpture that goes from the playground to the dinner table without asking permission.

Coming soon Follow the launch

C Piece Nº 03

Squirrels

A little friend on every step.

A squirrel curls over the toe, its tail sweeping back along the side, and a chunky sole keeps every adventure grounded. SQUIRRELS is pure storytelling in 3D print — the kind of shoe kids name, talk to, and never want to take off.
